Invitation to Participate

You are invited to participate in this study to help us understand how we evaluate other people's actions. You will be asked to read a series of scenarios and tell us if you believe the person's actions were right or wrong.

Voluntary Participation

Participation in this study is voluntary, and will take no more than 20 minutes total of your time. You will be reimbursed through Prolific upon completion of the study. By volunteering for this study, you will learn about research in psychology in general and the topic of human-AI interaction in particular. In addition, you will receive detailed feedback about the nature and purpose of this study. During this online study, you can withdraw at any time without loss of payment by progressing to the end without submitting your answers. However, once data has been collected, please note that researchers will have no way of knowing which data belongs to which participant due to the de-identification of data.

Risks and Benefits

The risks associated with participation in the study are expected to be no greater than what you might experience in your day to day life. There are no direct personal benefits to participation; however, the data collected will serve to further explain the factors influencing AI adoption. Further, you may decide to withdraw from this study at any time by clicking through to the end without answering without any penalty or loss of participation credit. However, once data has been collected, please note that researchers may have no way of knowing which data belongs to which participant due to the de-identification of data. By taking part, you will also gain first-hand experience with psychological research and receive a detailed debriefing about the study's purpose.

Privacy and Confidentiality

You will be completing this study online, and will be recruited and reimbursed through Prolific. When information is transmitted over the internet, privacy cannot be guaranteed. There is always a risk your responses may be intercepted by a third party (e.g., government agencies, hackers). Prolific collects your participant ID so that researchers can verify completion and issue payment, but we will not collect information that could identify you personally beyond what Prolific already requires for payment purposes. If you prefer not to submit your survey responses through the online platform, please do not participate in this study.

University of Toronto researchers will not collect or use IP addresses or other information which could link your participation to your computer or electronic device without first informing you. Your identity in this study will be deidentified; your Prolific ID will be collected only to verify completion and issue payment, and will not be included or in any other way associated with the de-identified data analyzed by the research team. Your responses will be securely transmitted and stored using DataPipe, a secure data-relay service built for behavioural research, and then archived on the Open Science Framework (OSF), a password-protected, access-controlled repository. All de-identified data will be retained for a minimum of seven (7) years. Furthermore, all de-identified data may be posted to an online repository to help facilitate the ongoing reproducibility effort within Psychological Science.

Questions and Ethics Clearance

This study has been reviewed and received ethics clearance through a University of Toronto Research Ethics Board (REB Protocol #50735). If you have questions, contact the Research Oversight and Compliance Office – Human Research Ethics Program at ethics.review@utoronto.ca or 416-946-3273.

If you have any questions about the study, please contact Pelin Tan, pelin.tan@utoronto.ca.

Consent to Participate

I have read the information presented in the consent letter about the study being conducted under the supervision of Dr. Michael Inzlicht of the Department of Psychology at the University of Toronto, ON, Canada. I have had the opportunity to contact the Researchers with any questions related to this study, and have received satisfactory answers to my questions, and any additional details I wanted. I am aware that I may withdraw from the study without loss of participation remuneration at any time by advising the researcher prior to the scheduled participation meeting or during the meeting.

  • My participation is voluntary and I may withdraw at any time without penalty; however, if I withdraw before completing the study I will not receive the Prolific completion code and will not be compensated.
  • My responses will be de-identified and I cannot be identified in any published results.
  • Once my data have been submitted and de-identified, I am not able to withdraw from the study as the researchers have no way of knowing which de-identified data belonged to me.
  • If I have questions about my rights as a participant, I can contact the University of Toronto Research Oversight and Compliance Office – Human Research Ethics Program at ethics.review@utoronto.ca or 416-946-3273.
  • By agreeing to participate, I am not waiving any of my legal rights, and I am not releasing the investigators or the University of Toronto from their legal and professional responsibilities.
